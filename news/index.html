    February 2026
    A study I led during my internship at Microsoft Research has been accepted to CHI 2026! We worked with 20 science journalists to uncover the ways in which differnt kinds of LLM-infused sensemaking tools might impact their values, practices and hence their agency. Our work offers both design and theoretical implications that follow from this. Find the paper here, and find me in Barcelona at CHI this April!

    June 2025
    Our paper on value-sensitive design for journalism has been accepted to the ACM DIS 2025 conference, and also won a Best Paper Honorable Mention award! The paper explores how we can design sociotechnical systems for journalism that support journalists’ values, and has some fun discussion of the value tensions and trade-offs that arise in the design process.

    I’ll be co-organizing the workshop on News Futures at CHI 2025 in Yokohama, Japan! We’ll explore the design of sociotechnical systems for news production and consumption, and hopefully develop new research ideas for the field going ahead!
    April 2025
    Our short paper titled Designing for Agency in LLM-Infused Writing Support Tools for Science Journalism has been accepted to the workshop on Tools for Thought at CHI 2025! This work comes out of my internship at MSR FATE last summer; stay tuned for more on this over the next year.
    March 2025
    I’ve officially passed my prospectus exam! My dissertation will focus on different ways of designing creativity support tools for journalists, and how they expand our theoretical notions of creativity in this domain.
