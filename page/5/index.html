    Hi, I’m Sachita! I’m a 5th-year PhD candidate at Northwestern University, advised by Dr. Nick Diakopoulos. My research asks: how can systems for navigating, parsing, and creating with information on the internet support people’s values and practices?

    I address this question in the domain of science journalism, where I design interactive AI-infused systems to support how journalists explore, make sense of, and translate large-scale scientific information during news production. New technologies like generative AI introduce value trade-offs—accuracy versus efficiency, independence versus algorithmic support—that reshape workflows and professional identity. My research surfaces such value trade-offs and proposes design patterns to help journalists navigate these more thoughtfully and intentionally, in the interest of accurate, timely, useful science communication.
