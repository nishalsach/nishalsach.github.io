    Hi, I’m Sachita! I’m a 5th-year PhD candidate at Northwestern University, advised by Dr. Nick Diakopoulos. My research asks: how can systems for navigating, parsing, and creating with information on the internet support people’s values and practices?

    I address this question in the domain of science journalism, where I design interactive AI-infused systems to support journalists’ exploration, sensemaking, and communication of large-scale scientific information. New technologies like generative AI introduce value trade-offs—accuracy versus efficiency, independence versus algorithmic support—that reshape workflows and professional identity.

    Through computational techniques that operationalize news values and interactive systems that reveal how AI impacts practice, my work demonstrates that effective sensemaking systems must uphold what makes knowledge work meaningful: professional standards, public mission, and personal rewards like agency and creativity. I extend this empirical work into theory and evaluation frameworks that help designers navigate value tensions in AI systems.
